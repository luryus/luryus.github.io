Wattiviisari – electricity prices on your smartwatch

Wattiviisari brings Finnish electricity spot prices to your smartwatch. View today's and tomorrow's electricity prices directly in the watch app.

Get Wattiviisari on Google Play

Electricity prices and automatic updates

Wattiviisari updates price information automatically. Tomorrow's prices appear once they have been published and fetched by the app.

The app shows electricity spot prices for Finland. The primary data source is the ENTSO-E transparency platform. An alternative source is the Elering.ee API.

The electricity price on your watch face

Add Wattiviisari's Electricity price now complication to check the current price at a glance.

To add the price to your watch face:

  1. Open your watch face's editor and choose a complication slot.
  2. Select Wattiviisari's Electricity price now complication.
  3. Save your watch face changes.

Install on your smartwatch

Wattiviisari supports all Wear OS smartwatches, including Samsung Galaxy Watch and Google Pixel Watch. Install the app on your watch from Google Play.

Privacy

Wattiviisari does not collect user data. Read the privacy policy.
